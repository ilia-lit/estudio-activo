Gramática A2 → B1

Cada unidad tiene la fórmula, ejemplos con audio, un truco y el error típico de los hispanohablantes. Después practícala en 🎯.

U1 Verbo TO BE (ser y estar)

I am · you/we/they are · he/she/it is

I am an accounting student. She is tired. They are at the bank.

💜 Truco: en inglés la edad, el hambre, el frío y la razón van con BE, no con “have”: I am 25 years old. I am hungry. You are right.

❌ I have 25 years → ✅ I am 25 years old

U2 Presente simple (rutinas y hechos)

I/you/we/they work · he/she/it works · Negativa: don't / doesn't + verbo · Pregunta: Do / Does … ?

She works at a bank. I don't drink coffee. Does he study finance?

💜 “La S de SHE”: con he, she o it el verbo lleva -s. Cuando aparece does, la S se pasa al auxiliar y el verbo queda limpio: Does she work? (no “works”).

❌ She work here · ❌ Does she works? → ✅ She works here · ✅ Does she work?

U3 Presente continuo (ahora mismo)

am / is / are + verbo-ing

I am studying right now. They are having a meeting.

💜 Simple = siempre · Continuo = ahora: I work at a bank (es mi trabajo) frente a I'm working from home today (solo hoy).

Verbos de estado no van en -ing: know, like, want, need, believe. ❌ I'm knowing → ✅ I know

U4 Pasado simple

Regulares: verbo + ed (worked) · Irregulares: de memoria (went had paid)

Negativa y pregunta: didn't / Did + verbo BASE

I paid the invoice yesterday. Did you go to class? We didn't finish the report.

💜 “DID se roba el pasado”: cuando aparece did, el verbo vuelve a su forma base. ❌ Did you went? → ✅ Did you go?
U5 Pasado continuo

was / were + verbo-ing: una acción en progreso en el pasado, que muchas veces es interrumpida.

I was studying when my phone rang.

💜 La película y el flash: la acción larga (was studying) es la película; la corta (rang) es el flash que la interrumpe.
U6 Futuro: will · going to · presente continuo

will = decisión del momento, promesa o predicción: I'll call you later.

going to = plan ya decidido: I'm going to take the C1 exam.

presente continuo = cita agendada: I'm meeting my boss on Monday.

💜 Will = impulso · Going to = agenda.
U7 Present perfect (la más difícil para hispanohablantes)

have / has + participio

  • Experiencias: Have you ever been to New York?
  • Algo que empezó en el pasado y sigue: I have lived here for five years.
  • Resultado que importa ahora: I have finished the report.

for = duración (for 2 years) · since = punto de inicio (since 2020) · just, already, yet.

💜 Puente pasado → presente: si hay un momento terminado (yesterday, in 2020, last week), usa pasado simple. Si la frase llega hasta hoy, usa present perfect. “Llevo 5 años aquí” = I have been here for 5 years.

❌ I live here since 2020 → ✅ I have lived here since 2020

U8 Comparativos y superlativos

Cortos: -er / the -est (cheaper, the cheapest) · Largos: more / the most (more expensive)

Irregulares: good, better, the best · bad, worse, the worst

❌ more cheap · ❌ more better → ✅ cheaper · ✅ better

U9 Contables e incontables

many + contables (many clients) · much + incontables (much money) · a lot of sirve para los dos.

Incontables que confunden: information, advice, money, news, homework, furniture. No llevan -s.

💜 ❌ informations, advices → ✅ information, a piece of advice
U10 Modales: can · could · should · must · have to

I can speak English. (habilidad) · Could you help me? (petición educada) · You should rest. (consejo) · You must sign here. (obligación fuerte) · I have to work tomorrow. (obligación externa)

💜 Después de un modal, verbo limpio: sin “to” y sin -s. ❌ She can speaks · ❌ You should to go

Ojo: mustn't = está prohibido · don't have to = no es necesario

U11 Condicionales 0 y 1

Cero (verdades): If + presente, presente: If you heat water, it boils.

Primero (posible y real): If + presente, will + verbo: If I study every day, I will reach C1.

💜 Nunca pongas will después de if: ❌ If I will study → ✅ If I study
U12 Gerundio (-ing) o infinitivo (to)

Con -ing: enjoy, finish, avoid, mind, keep, suggest y después de preposiciones: I enjoy reading. I'm interested in learning.

Con to: want, need, decide, hope, plan, would like: I want to improve my English.

💜 look forward to + -ing: I look forward to hearing from you. Aquí el “to” es preposición, no infinitivo.
U13 Hacer preguntas

Palabra interrogativa + auxiliar + sujeto + verbo

Where do you work? What did she say? How long have you studied English?

💜 QASV: Question word · Auxiliar · Sujeto · Verbo. ❌ Where you work? → ✅ Where do you work?
U14 Conectores (para sonar B1)

and, but, so, because · although (aunque) · however (sin embargo) · also · first, then, finally · for example

Although it was late, I finished the test. The test was hard. However, I passed.

💜 Para subir de nivel, no basta con saber más palabras: hay que unir ideas. Una persona en A2 habla en frases sueltas; una en B1 las conecta.
